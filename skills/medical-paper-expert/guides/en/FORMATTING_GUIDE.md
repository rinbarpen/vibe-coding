# English Paper Formatting & Typography

## 1. Common Citation Styles

### Vancouver Style (Common in Medicine)
- **In-text**: Superscript numbers or numbers in brackets [1].
- **Reference List**: Numbered list in order of appearance.
- **Example**: 1. Smith A, Jones B. Title of article. *Journal Name*. 2024;10(2):123-130.

### AMA Style (American Medical Association)
- **In-text**: Superscript numbers.
- **Reference List**: Similar to Vancouver but with specific punctuation rules.
- **Example**: 1. Smith A, Jones B. Title of article. *Abbrev J Name*. 2024;10(2):123-130. doi:10.1001/jama.2024.1

### APA Style (Social Sciences / Nursing)
- **In-text**: Author-date (Smith & Jones, 2024).
- **Reference List**: Alphabetical by author.

## 2. Figures and Tables

### Tables
- **Three-line format**: Only horizontal lines at the top, bottom, and below column headers. No vertical lines.
- **Title**: Above the table.
- **Footnotes**: Below the table, explaining abbreviations and statistical symbols (*, †, ‡).

### Figures
- **Resolution**: 
    - Line art: 1200 DPI.
    - Grayscale/Halftone: 600 DPI.
    - Color: 300 DPI.
- **Fonts**: Use sans-serif fonts like Arial or Helvetica for text inside figures.
- **Scale Bars**: Mandatory for micrographs.

## 3. Typography & Units

### Spacing and Alignment
- **Line Spacing**: Double-spaced (standard for submission).
- **Margins**: 1-inch (2.54 cm) on all sides.
- **Alignment**: Left-aligned (ragged right) or justified (depending on journal).

### Units of Measurement
- Use **SI units** (e.g., kg, m, s, L).
- Use a non-breaking space between the number and unit (e.g., 10 mg, not 10mg).
- **Exception**: No space before % or ° (e.g., 95%, 37°C).

### Mathematical Notation
- Use italics for variables (e.g., *P*, *n*, *t*, *F*).
- Use standard LaTeX for complex equations:
    - $SD = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n-1}}$
